Privacy Policy — Mastward

Last updated: 2026-11-16 · AXD Intelligence · Designed by Diego Nanini

Short version: Mastward runs on your own computer and your own phone. We do not collect your data. The only thing that ever passes through a server of ours is an iPhone alert you have switched on, and only so it can reach your phone.

What we collect

Nothing about you. Mastward has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking SDKs, and no account on our servers. We do not see, receive or store:

  • your terminal sessions, commands, output or scrollback;
  • your keys, passwords, tokens or files;
  • your photos or camera images;
  • your computer names, addresses, or which devices you connect to;
  • any usage data.

Where your data lives

  • Your sessions run on your computer (the Mastward engine you install). History is stored locally on that computer.
  • Your phone talks directly to your own computer, over your home Wi-Fi or another connection you already have. Pairing needs your approval on the computer.
  • Camera and photos are used only when you choose to: to scan a pairing code, or to send a picture from your phone to your own computer. They are never sent to us.
  • Device keys and any licence key are stored on your devices and checked offline.

Alerts on iPhone (optional)

If you turn on alerts on an iPhone, your computer sends the alert's title and text, together with your phone's Apple push token, through AXD's notification relay to Apple's push service, which delivers it to your phone. This is used only to deliver that alert. You can keep alert text private by leaving alerts off, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings → Alerts. Android does not use this relay.

Payment

If you buy a licence, payment is handled by the app store you bought it from, or directly between you and us. The app itself sends no payment data.

Third parties

Mastward includes open-source components (see Third_party_notices.md); none of them collect or send your data. We do not sell or share data.

Children

Mastward is not directed at children under 13 (or 16, where local law says so).

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Deleting the app removes its data from your phone; uninstalling the engine removes it from your computer. Questions: support@axdintelligence.com.au.
